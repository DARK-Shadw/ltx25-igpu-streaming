High-budget Japanese action anime film, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, deep cel-shaded shadows and crimson rim lighting over richly painted widescreen backgrounds. A burning city street at night in heavy rain, orange fire glowing through the broken windows of ruined buildings, black smoke and glowing embers drifting upward. At the far end of the street the towering eight-foot demon warlord with charcoal-grey skin, two curved black horns, glowing orange eyes, heavy black iron armor split by glowing orange cracks and a huge spiked iron war-hammer, drawn in the same hand-drawn cel style with bold black ink outlines stands motionless on a heap of rubble, silhouetted against the fire, the hammer resting on his shoulder. The camera begins in a wide shot and pushes in slowly and steadily as he turns his head toward the viewer and his orange eyes ignite. In a deep rumbling voice he says: "So the last blade finally comes to me." The sound is a low rumbling growl, heavy rain, crackling fire and a slow deep drum beat.
