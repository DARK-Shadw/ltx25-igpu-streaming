High-budget Japanese action anime film, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, deep cel-shaded shadows and crimson rim lighting over richly painted widescreen backgrounds. In a burning city street at night in heavy rain, orange fire glowing through the broken windows of ruined buildings, black smoke and glowing embers drifting upward, the towering eight-foot demon warlord with charcoal-grey skin, two curved black horns, glowing orange eyes, heavy black iron armor split by glowing orange cracks and a huge spiked iron war-hammer, drawn in the same hand-drawn cel style with bold black ink outlines kneels on the wet pavement, the hammer fallen beside him, bright orange cracks of light spreading across his body and armor, while the tall lone swordsman with messy silver hair, a long vertical scar across his left eye, sharp crimson eyes, a long black coat with a torn crimson lining, black gloves and a katana with a faintly glowing red blade stands in front of him with his back to the camera as it pushes in slowly from behind. In a weak, deep rumble the warlord says: "At last... silence." The sound is a fading growl, crackling energy, steady rain and a slow low drum.
