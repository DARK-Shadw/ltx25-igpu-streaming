High-budget Japanese action anime film, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, deep cel-shaded shadows and crimson rim lighting over richly painted widescreen backgrounds. The tall lone swordsman with messy silver hair, a long vertical scar across his left eye, sharp crimson eyes, a long black coat with a torn crimson lining, black gloves and a katana with a faintly glowing red blade, in a burning city street at night in heavy rain, orange fire glowing through the broken windows of ruined buildings, black smoke and glowing embers drifting upward. He lowers himself slowly into a low fighting stance with both feet in a puddle, grips the katana with both gloved hands and the red blade glows brighter and brighter, while the camera circles slowly around him at waist height. The sound is a rising electric hum of the blade, steady rain, crackling fire and a slow tense drum.
