High-budget Japanese action anime film, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, deep cel-shaded shadows and crimson rim lighting over richly painted widescreen backgrounds. A burning city street at night in heavy rain, orange fire glowing through the broken windows of ruined buildings, black smoke and glowing embers drifting upward. The tall lone swordsman has messy silver hair, a long vertical scar across his left eye, sharp crimson eyes, a long black coat with a torn crimson lining and a katana with a faintly glowing red blade; the towering demon warlord has charcoal-grey skin, two curved black horns, glowing orange eyes, heavy black iron armor split by glowing orange cracks and a huge spiked iron war-hammer; both are drawn in the same bold-ink cel style. The swordsman charges forward across the wet street, leaps and slashes the glowing red katana in a diagonal arc as the warlord swings the huge hammer down to meet him, and the blade and hammer collide in a burst of crimson and orange sparks that explodes outward, forcing the warlord back a step as he slides across the wet pavement. The camera starts low, tracks around them in a quick half-circle, then settles into a slow push-in on the clash. Dynamic speed lines and a subtle motion smear on the fast strikes, rain and embers whipping through the air. The sound is a sharp metallic clang, a rushing whoosh of the blade, a heavy low impact boom and pounding drums.
