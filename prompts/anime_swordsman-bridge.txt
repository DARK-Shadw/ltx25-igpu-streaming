High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. A teenage swordsman with spiky black hair, a small scar on his forehead, determined red-brown eyes, a dark navy uniform with gold buttons and a flowing white-and-crimson haori stands on an old stone bridge in a snowy mountain village at dusk, warm orange lantern light glowing in the wooden windows behind him. He plants his feet, draws a katana with a bright silver blade in one smooth motion and slashes diagonally, and a swirling ribbon of blue water with white foam and ink-wash brush strokes follows the blade through the air and spirals around him. The camera starts at a low angle, tracks around him in a quick half-circle, then settles into a slow push-in on his focused face as his hair and haori whip in the wind. Dynamic speed lines and a subtle motion smear on the fast sword stroke, crisp painted sky in deep purple and orange. The sound is a sharp metallic draw of the blade, a rushing whoosh of water and soft wind.
