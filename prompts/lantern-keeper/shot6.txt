High-budget Japanese anime film, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, soft cel shading and richly painted backgrounds, a cool teal and indigo night palette with warm amber lantern light and crimson accents. Starting on the swordsman's calm face, the camera slowly pulls back and rises to reveal an ancient stone stairway climbing to a mountain temple gate at night in heavy rain, rows of mossy stone lanterns along the steps, dark pine trees and low clouds as the rain thins and the first golden light of dawn breaks over the mountains; the small girl in the yellow raincoat walks ahead with her lantern and the tall swordsman follows one step behind, and the stone lanterns along the steps ignite one by one in a warm glow. The camera continues to rise smoothly above them. The sound is soft rain fading to birdsong, the gentle whoosh of lanterns lighting and a warm, swelling piano and strings melody.
