# The Lantern Keeper (30 s anime short)
Art style (identical first sentence in every shot): hand-drawn cel animation, bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, soft cel shading, painted backgrounds, teal/indigo night palette with amber lantern light and crimson accents.
Characters (descriptions repeated verbatim in every shot): a small girl (copper-orange bob, big brown eyes, yellow raincoat, paper lantern) and a tall silver-haired swordsman (vertical scar over left eye, crimson eyes, black coat with torn crimson lining, katana).
Story: at night in the rain a small girl guards a mountain temple stairway with her lantern; a scarred swordsman climbs toward her; she refuses to move aside, her courage softens him, he sheathes his sword and they climb to the temple together as dawn breaks.
Script lines: 1 girl "He's coming." | 3 swordsman "Move aside, child." | 4 girl "No. The whole village is behind me." | 5 swordsman "Then lead the way."
Shots chain from frames: 1 text only | 2 from last frame of 1 | 3 from 2 | 4 from 1 (cut back to the girl) | 5 from 3 (cut to the swordsman) | 6 from 5 (pull back and rise).
