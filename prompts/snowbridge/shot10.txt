High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. At dusk on the snowy bridge, the teenage swordsman lowers his katana and breathes hard, snow settling on his shoulders and haori as the first stars appear, while the camera pushes in slowly on his tired but calm face. In a low voice he says: "It is over. Go home." The sound is heavy breathing, soft wind and quiet strings returning.
