High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. In a medium shot on an old stone bridge over a frozen stream in a snowy mountain village at dusk, warm orange lantern light glowing in the wooden windows, soft snow falling, a deep purple and orange sky. The teenage swordsman has spiky black hair, a small scar on his forehead, a dark navy uniform with gold buttons, a flowing white-and-crimson haori and a bright silver katana; the tall rival has long white hair tied back, cold violet eyes, a black high-collared coat with purple trim and a black katana wrapped in purple flame; both are drawn in the same bold-ink cel style. The swordsman steps forward and slashes diagonally, a swirling ribbon of blue water with white foam following his blade, and the rival parries with a controlled sweep of his black katana trailing purple flame; blade and flame meet in a burst of blue and purple sparks and the rival slides back one step across the snow. The camera starts at a low angle and tracks slowly around them in a half-circle, then settles into a slow push-in. Snow and glowing sparks drift through the air. The sound is a sharp metallic clang, a rushing whoosh of water, a low impact boom and pounding drums.
