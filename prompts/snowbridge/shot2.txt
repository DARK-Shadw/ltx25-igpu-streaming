High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. On the old stone bridge at dusk, the camera keeps gliding in until the teenage swordsman fills the frame in a medium shot. He slowly turns his head toward the far end of the bridge, rests his hand on the hilt of his katana and narrows his determined red-brown eyes while snow drifts past and his white-and-crimson haori flutters. In a calm, low voice he says: "I knew you would come at dusk." The sound is soft wind, quiet strings and a distant temple bell.
