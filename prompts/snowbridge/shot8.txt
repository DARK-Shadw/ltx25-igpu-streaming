High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. Mid-duel on the snowy bridge at dusk, in a medium shot the white-haired rival in the black coat with purple trim steps back with snow spraying from his boots and sweeps his black katana trailing purple flame, the teenage swordsman in the white-and-crimson haori ducks under it, turns and slashes upward, and a swirling ribbon of blue water with white foam rises and wraps around the black blade. The camera moves in a slow, smooth arc around them and then pushes in gently. Snow and glowing sparks drift through the air. The sound is metallic clashes, a rushing whoosh of water, the hiss of flame and a steady drum.
