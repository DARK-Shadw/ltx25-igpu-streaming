High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. An old stone bridge over a frozen stream in a snowy mountain village at dusk, warm orange lantern light glowing in the wooden windows, soft snow falling, a deep purple and orange sky. The teenage swordsman has spiky black hair, a small scar on his forehead, a dark navy uniform with gold buttons, a flowing white-and-crimson haori and a bright silver katana; the tall rival has long white hair tied back, cold violet eyes, a black high-collared coat with purple trim and a black katana wrapped in purple flame; both are drawn in the same bold-ink cel style. The swordsman charges across the bridge, leaps and slashes diagonally, and a swirling ribbon of blue water with white foam follows his blade as the rival meets him with a sweeping slash trailing purple flame, and blade and flame collide in a burst of blue and purple sparks that explodes outward, sliding the rival back across the snow. The camera starts low, tracks around them in a quick half-circle, then settles into a slow push-in. Dynamic speed lines and a subtle motion smear on the fast strikes, snow whipping through the air. The sound is a sharp metallic clang, a rushing whoosh of water, a low impact boom and pounding drums.
