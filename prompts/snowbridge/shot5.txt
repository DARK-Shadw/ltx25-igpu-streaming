High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. At dusk on the snowy bridge, the teenage swordsman stands his ground and draws his katana in one smooth motion, the bright silver blade catching the warm lantern light as a thin ribbon of blue water curls around it, while the camera pushes in slowly on his determined face. In a firm voice he says: "Not while I am standing." The sound is a sharp metallic draw of the blade, a soft rush of water and wind.
