High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. At dusk on the snowy bridge, the tall white-haired rival with cold violet eyes raises his gaze to the viewer and a thin smile forms on his pale face as purple flame flickers along his black katana, while the camera pushes in slowly and steadily on his face. In a smooth, cold voice he says: "This village ends tonight." The sound is low wind, the soft crackle of purple flame and a slow, deep drum.
