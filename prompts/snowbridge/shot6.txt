High-budget Japanese TV action anime, hand-drawn cel animation with bold black ink outlines, flat saturated colors, two-step cel shading and richly painted backgrounds. An old stone bridge over a frozen stream in a snowy mountain village at dusk, warm orange lantern light glowing in the wooden windows, soft snow falling, a deep purple and orange sky. The teenage swordsman stands on the left and the tall rival with long white hair tied back, cold violet eyes and a black coat with purple trim stands on the right, ten paces apart on the bridge, both with drawn blades: his bright silver katana wrapped in a thin ribbon of blue water, the rival's black katana wrapped in purple flame. Both are drawn in the same bold-ink cel style. The camera tracks slowly sideways as snow whirls between them and their coats flutter. The sound is wind, a tense, slow drum and the faint hiss of flame and water.
